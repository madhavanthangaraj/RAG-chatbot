# Reset Student Password Guide

To reset your ERP Student Portal password, please follow these steps:
1. Navigate to the portal login page at [ERP Portal Login](http://localhost:3000/login).
2. Click on the **'Forgot Password'** link located below the login button.
3. Enter your Registered Student Email ID or Student Roll Number.
4. Check your inbox for the password reset link.
5. Click the link and enter your new password (must be at least 8 characters and include 1 number and 1 special character).


If you do not receive the email, check your Spam folder or contact the IT Helpdesk at helpdesk@university.edu.
