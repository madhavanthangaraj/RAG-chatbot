# Submitting Feedback & Suggestions

We value your input. To submit feedback regarding your classes, campus facilities, or ERP portal:
1. Log in to your ERP Dashboard.
2. Navigate to the **'Feedback & Survey'** tab on the left sidebar menu.
3. Choose the type of feedback you wish to provide:
   - *Academic Course Evaluation* (available end of each semester)
   - *Campus Facilities & Mess Feedback*
   - *ERP System Bug Reports / Suggestions*
4. Fill out the rating stars and write down your comments.
5. Click **'Submit Feedback'** to log your response.

Your responses are used to improve campus operations. General feedback can be anonymous.
