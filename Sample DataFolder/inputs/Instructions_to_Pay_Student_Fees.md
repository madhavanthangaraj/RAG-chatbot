# Instructions to Pay Student Fees

Follow these official instructions to pay your tuition, hostel, or examination fees online:
1. Access the Student Portal and navigate to the **'Fee Payment Hub'**.
2. Review your pending dues statement shown on the dashboard.
3. Click on the **'Pay Outstanding Balance'** button.
4. Select your preferred payment mode:
   - *UPI* (Instant & No Transaction Charge)
   - *Net Banking* (All major banks supported)
   - *Credit/Debit Card* (1% transaction fee applies)
5. Complete the payment gateway authorization.
6. Once payment is processed, you will be redirected to download your **Official Fee Receipt** in PDF format.

*Note:* Fees paid via NEFT/IMPS bank transfer may take up to 48 hours to reconcile. Always keep a copy of your receipt.
